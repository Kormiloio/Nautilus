# Montenegrin vocabulary expansion: days 24–40

**170 new words. Every day: 10 new + 10 review = 20 distinct words.**

Status: authored draft; fluent Montenegrin review and audio approval remain pending. These entries have no invented reviewer or approval metadata. New-word checks cover exact legacy tokens and recorded aliases; linguistic equivalence still needs fluent review.

In the updated local app, open the adult **Preview activities** screen and choose a day under **Vocabulary set**. Flashcards, matching and quizzes use that day’s vocabulary. The word list identifies New and Review. These preview interactions do not write progress.

The continuation follows the reported day-23 position. Review starts from earlier Family, Numbers and Colors vocabulary. Subsequent days combine five recent words with five older words. The deployed course is not modified by this draft.
