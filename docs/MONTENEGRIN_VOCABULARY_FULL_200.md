# Montenegrin: complete 200-day vocabulary draft

2,010 distinct lexical entries. Day 1 introduces 20 words; each later day introduces 10 words and reviews 10 earlier words. All language remains draft pending fluent review. This is a new-course authoring plan; it does not rewrite the legacy progress of the current family. Live pilot days 24–40 retain their separate legacy review baseline.
