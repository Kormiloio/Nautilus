# Historical prototype specification

**Status:** Archived baseline; non-normative

This document records the observable behavior of the original Claude-generated export preserved at `web/prototype.html`. It is retained for historical comparison only. It does not describe approved production behavior and must not be used as a current product contract.

## Historical behaviors

- Four hard-coded profiles: Lena, Sam, Mario, and Kristina.
- Weekly topic unlocks beginning August 9, 2026.
- An August-through-July twelve-month journey.
- Profile selection advanced the stored streak before learning occurred.
- Browser speech synthesis and optional temporary microphone recording.

The production replacement supersedes weekly locks, the twelve-month journey, and profile-selection streaks with free topic browsing, a 200-lesson voyage, and activity-based progress.
