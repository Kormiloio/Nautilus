# Daily net-new vocabulary

## Why
The family at day 23 reports repeated familiar words and five-minute lessons that are too easy. Activity reshuffling does not provide vocabulary growth.

## Change
Require at least half of every core day's distinct vocabulary to be new to the language curriculum, with a configurable initial target of 20 words. Introduce canonical lexical identities, explicit reviewed daily allocations, common individual/family generation, and a publication gate. Preserve old attempts and progress.

## Implications
This requires substantial new authored vocabulary and fluent review for every pack. Audio retains existing approved/fallback policies. No new learner data is collected. Existing accessible activity components are reused. Production catalog publication follows content approval; draft generation is not approval.
