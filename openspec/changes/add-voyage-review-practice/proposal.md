# Proposal: Add Review from Your Voyage practice

## Why

Nautilus already brings back earlier words inside authored voyage lessons. Families also need an optional, self-directed way to revisit language that has become less familiar, especially words or sentence activities answered incorrectly. This should feel like a useful short practice choice, not a remediation label, score chase, or penalty.

## Learner and family outcome

A learner can open **Review from your voyage** and receive a small, varied practice set drawn from language they have already encountered. A family can use the same reviewed content together without changing the shared Family Voyage position.

## What changes

- Add an optional language-scoped Review from your voyage entry point on the learner dashboard and practice area.
- Select items from verified learning history using transparent review signals: prior incorrect graded responses, longest time since successful recall, and reasonable topic variety.
- Reuse existing Flashcards, Match, Quiz, Listen, sentence builder, and sentence completion only when their authored content and prerequisites are available.
- Show a short explanation of why material returned, such as “bringing back words from earlier voyages.”
- Keep all review sessions optional and separate from the shared Family Voyage.

## Content, privacy, accessibility, and audio

- Only published, reviewed, language-pack content and pinned catalog revisions may appear. No machine-generated sentences or inferred grammar are introduced.
- Review selection uses the learner's existing language-scoped verified receipts and completion history. It does not expose one learner's performance to another learner or adult by default.
- Every review activity remains keyboard and touch operable, works without audio, honors reduced motion, and uses clear textual feedback rather than color alone.

## Non-goals

- No timers, lives, Hearts, Energy, public leaderboard, streak loss, shame messaging, or forced remedial route.
- No speech recognition, free-text grading, or new AI service.
- No Family Voyage-day advancement or automatic adult notification.

## Unresolved product decisions

- Whether a family adult can later create a shared review playlist from selected topics.
- Whether reviewed answers should use a fixed spacing interval or an adaptive scheduler after sufficient evidence is collected.
