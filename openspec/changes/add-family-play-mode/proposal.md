# Proposal: Add parent-controlled Family Play Mode

## Why

The current application conflates activity completion with calendar-day progress. A learner can finish several activities in one sitting and appear to advance several voyage days. Learners can also move ahead independently even though the core product goal is a family sitting together, speaking aloud, and progressing as one group.

## What changes

- Count a calendar learning date once, regardless of completed activity count.
- Separate the shared Family Voyage from Independent Practice.
- Let an authorized adult start and control a live Family Play session.
- Let selected learners join the same session from their devices.
- Credit one shared family day only when the adult completes the session.
- Present the shared route as an immersive moving voyage world.

## Compatibility

Existing completed lesson records remain useful as content-mastery history. Existing unique activity dates provide the provisional day count until explicit family-session records are deployed. No historical activity record is deleted.
