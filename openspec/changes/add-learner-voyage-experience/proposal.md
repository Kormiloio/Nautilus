# Proposal: Add the learner voyage experience

## Why

The 200-day calendar communicates dates but does not give learners a memorable sense of movement or ownership. Nautilus needs a visual journey that connects daily lessons to the ten-month goal without introducing competitive rankings or punitive mechanics.

## What changes

- Replace the abstract ten-month timeline with a responsive ten-port voyage map.
- Express progress through five nautical milestone labels.
- Add a Captain's Quarters learner logbook.
- Award monthly language-passport stamps and sea companions from completed lesson records.
- Keep all voyage rewards isolated by learner and language pack.

## Scope

This change uses the existing local and cloud progress records. It does not add database tables, editable avatars, animation, social comparison, or new lesson-completion rules.
