# Proposal: Add a privacy-safe platform administration dashboard

## Why

Nautilus now supports multiple independent families. The operator needs enough visibility to understand adoption, deployment health, invitation failures, and Family Play reliability without turning private family learning into routine surveillance.

## Scope

- Add a platform role separate from family owner and adult-guide roles.
- Provide aggregate counts and trends for families, active families, enabled language packs, completed sessions, invitations, and operational failures.
- Provide an audited support workflow for exceptional family-specific troubleshooting.
- Exclude private vocabulary, learner answers, detailed lesson history, and child-level activity from the default dashboard.

## Out of scope

- Ranking families or learners.
- Reading private family content by default.
- Impersonating users.
- Marketing profiles or behavioral advertising.
